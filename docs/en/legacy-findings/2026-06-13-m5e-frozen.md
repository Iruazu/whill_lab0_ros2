# Freeze record: M5-e/Phase A-C (2026-06-13)

Language: [日本語](../../ja/legacy-findings/2026-06-13-m5e-frozen.md) | [English](2026-06-13-m5e-frozen.md)

## Freeze date and grounds

The freeze was officially executed on 2026-06-13 under Issue #28. The upstream
grounds are the prohibition items #1 and #2 in section 5 ("Rules of conduct
for Claude Code") of the policy document
[`../plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md`](../plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md),
together with the freeze declaration at the top of section 4 ("Milestone
redefinition").

Section 4 (`docs/en/plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md`) verbatim:

> The old M5-d / M5-e are frozen and replaced by the phases below. The
> detailed plan for each phase is produced by `pm-orchestrator` under
> `docs/plans/`, using this document as input, when the phase starts.

Section 5 (`docs/en/plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md`) verbatim
(prohibitions #1 and #2):

> 1. Adding new features that assume the identity-`tf_bridge_launch.py`
>    configuration (including continuing the old M5-d).
> 2. Reinforcing FAST-LIO as a runtime localizer. (Re-tuning parameters is
>    allowed only when the goal is to improve map-building quality.)

The three physical operations that "execute" the freeze were carried out in
Issue #28:

1. The annotated tag `legacy/m5e-phase-abc-2026-06-13` was attached to
   `origin/m5e/velodyne-self-filter` HEAD (`9b5be71`) and pushed to `origin`.
2. The untracked Phase C remnants that lingered in the main working tree
   (`src/whill_localization/config/navsat_transform.yaml` and
   `src/whill_localization/launch/navsat_transform_launch.py`) were deleted.
   They were never tracked by git, so plain `rm` was used.
3. This freeze record was created in parallel under
   `docs/ja/legacy-findings/` and `docs/en/legacy-findings/`.

## Why it was frozen

Policy document, section 0 "Background"
(`docs/en/plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md`) verbatim:

> The 2026-06 technical survey (SLAM / localization methods, Tsukuba
> Challenge finisher configurations, licensing, compute) and a diagnosis of
> the current implementation jointly establish the following:
>
> - The current M5-a TF bridge (`map -> camera_init` pinned to identity) is
>   an approximation that only holds under the demo conditions of "short
>   duration, attended operation, and start from the mapping origin."
> - As run3 demonstrated (FAST-LIO diverged when a pedestrian crossed the
>   sensor), this approximation is structurally incompatible with the real
>   goal of "operation in an environment with people moving through it."
> - Stacking M5-d (goal-following) and M5-e (tuning) on top of that
>   approximation is the "wrong direction" we suspected. We must stop adding
>   features and replace the foundation.

Policy document, section 2 "Current problems (diagnostic summary)" P1-P5
(`docs/en/plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md`) verbatim:

> | ID | Problem | Evidence | Conflicting requirements |
> |----|---------|----------|--------------------------|
> | P1 | No correction path for runtime self-localization. With `map -> camera_init` pinned to identity, FAST-LIO drift (measured 18% / 60s) becomes map-frame error directly. | `tf_bridge_launch.py` | R2, R3 |
> | P2 | No initial-pose alignment mechanism. Because camera_init equals the startup pose, the chair is offset from the start unless it boots at the mapping origin in the same orientation. | `tf_bridge_launch.py`, the origin assumption in `pcd_to_occupancy_grid.py` | R1 |
> | P3 | Divergence is neither detected nor recovered. A single pedestrian can cause divergence (run3 measured), yet TF keeps flowing and Nav2 keeps driving. | `whill_localization/README.md` run3 | R4 |
> | P4 | No odom frame; the wheel odometry is unused. There is no cushion against jumps when correction is introduced, and no backup when LiDAR degrades. | `nav2_params.yaml` comments, CLAUDE.md known issues | R3, R4 |
> | P5 | Map-quality issues are cascading to disable safety features. Ghost obstacles -> `use_collision_detection: false`; QoS mismatch -> obstacle layer absent. Pedestrians during operation never appear in the costmap. | `nav2_params.yaml` comments | R5 |

Why Phase A's dual EKF cannot structurally resolve P1 and P3 is recorded
below. This explicitly rejects the survival argument "maybe Phase A only
needs a little more work to become equivalent to M4-R":

- Phase A assembled the TF hierarchy `map -> odom -> base_link` but had no
  correction source (scan-to-map matching) to drive the `map -> odom`
  transform. Inside Phase A this was approximated by feeding the FAST-LIO
  output into `ekf_map`, but that is precisely the path policy section 3.2
  prohibits as "reinforcing FAST-LIO as a runtime localizer." It does not
  structurally resolve P1 (absence of a correction path).
- Phase A has zero divergence-detection logic and no failsafe. Even when the
  EKF covariance blows up, `cmd_vel` is not gated and Nav2 keeps driving.
  That is exactly P3's "TF keeps flowing and Nav2 keeps driving" state,
  reproduced inside Phase A itself.
- These two points cannot be fixed by "additive" extensions on top of
  Phase A. M6-R needs to assemble a different architecture (a scan-to-map
  localizer plus a failsafe node) from scratch. Phase A got "the shape of
  the TF hierarchy" right, but "the responsibility split inside it" does
  not align with the policy.

Policy document section 5 ("Rules of conduct for Claude Code")
(`docs/en/plans/2026-06-11-platform-pivot.md`) codifies the above judgment
as an "absolute rule." Adding features that assume the identity
`tf_bridge_launch.py` configuration (prohibition #1) and reinforcing
FAST-LIO as a runtime localizer (prohibition #2) are both forbidden, and an
extension of Phase A-C runs into one or both of them.

## What remains

After the freeze, the references that let us re-examine why Phase A-C was
not adopted in the future are preserved as follows:

- The `origin/m5e/velodyne-self-filter` branch. Not deleted in this Issue.
  It keeps the Phase A-C HEAD reachable as a branch ref, including from the
  branch picker in editors / IDEs.
- The `legacy/m5e-phase-abc-2026-06-13` tag created by this Issue. Even if
  the branch is later force-pushed or renamed, the commit reference is
  pinned by the tag.

  series across branches and tags in one query.
